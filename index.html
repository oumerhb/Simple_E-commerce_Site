About Habeshan Treasures

Habeshan Treasures brings authentic Ethiopian cultural products directly to your doorstep. Founded in 2020, our mission is to share Ethiopia's rich heritage with the world while supporting local artisans and farmers.

Every product in our collection is carefully sourced from Ethiopian artisans who use traditional methods passed down through generations.
